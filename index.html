BATTERY MULTI-SCALE TWIN // FROM MOLECULES TO PACK LIFE

Concept demo for the proposal Manufacturing Memory Across Scales and Stages. Move an upstream knob — binder chemistry, drying, calendering — and watch its effect travel electrode → cell → pack → service life. Trained-model edition: the life stage is a multi-fidelity neural network (SEI physics fused with NASA Ames aging data, after Kohtz et al. 2022, RaDA Lab) and the electrode stage a simulation-trained neural surrogate structured after Galvez-Aranda et al. (2024). All numbers are computed live in your browser by the trained models — no server. Honest labels in the model notes below. Companion to the HydroTwin / TwinView digital twin work.
